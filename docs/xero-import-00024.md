### The six 630 rows: SKIPPED, deliberately

`opening-balances.csv` has seven rows. One is journal 388 (above). The other six are
journal 651's inventory-opening pairs: 630 against 630, 80.00 / 100.00 / 140.00 each way,
plus their 840 counterparts. They are **not posted**, because:

* each pair debits and credits 630 by the same amount, so the pair moves nothing;
* Xero prints **no 630 line** on its Trial Balance — `docs/xero-reference/trial-balance.txt`
  has 25 accounts and 630 is not one of them (reconciliation.md §5 says the same: 630 is in
  the GL Detail at 320.00 Dr / 320.00 Cr, net 0.00);
* `internal/handlers/report_render.go` renders a row for every account that has any figure,
  so a zero-net pair still leaves a non-zero debit and credit and would make goXero print a
  "630 Inventory" row Xero does not print;
* the posting table in `docs/xero-reference/reconciliation.md` §2 does not post them either.

This is a deliberate divergence from "post every row in the capture". It is the only one.

**The raw total is not 42,595.46 and is not forced to it.** The two figures measure
different things:

| figure | what it is |
|---|---|
| 108,392.54 | the raw sum of every signed `net_amount` in the organisation — every debit line and every credit line added up as it stands |
| 108,142.54 | the same over this migration's 156 journals |
| 42,595.46 | the Trial Balance: each of the 25 accounts **netted first**, then the positive balances and the negative balances summed |

The reason they differ is that the ledger is not netted: a sales account carries both the
invoice credit and the receipt debit, and summing its lines raw counts both. Summing the
lines of the 25 accounts without netting them gives 108,142.54 — the *remainder* of the other
33 accounts, i.e. the exact 630/877-type offset reconciliation.md §5 describes (Xero's GL
grand total is 111,263.08 against a Trial Balance total of 42,595.46, reconciliation.md
line 147). Before `00024` the raw organisation total was **30,839.16**.

## 3. No invented figures

Every value in the migration comes from a row in `migrations/data/xero/*.csv`. An empty CSV
cell becomes `NULL`, never 0. Cells left NULL, named:

| column | rows | why |
|---|---|---|
| `invoices.reference` | the bills whose `reference` cell is blank | the capture holds no reference |
| `invoices.invoice_number` | 5 bills | Xero leaves them blank; none is invented |
| `invoice_line_items.tax_type` | lines whose `tax_rate` cell is empty | nil rate is NULL, not `NONE` |
| `expense_claims.payment_due_date` | all 3 | the capture has no due date |
| `bank_transaction_line_items.tax_type` | the 26 settlement rows | a payment of a document carries no tax of its own |
| `contacts.email_address` | contacts with no captured email | the capture holds no address |
| `payments.invoice_id` / `credit_note_id` | 12 of 44 | the document number is blank or repeated for that contact, so the payment is left unattached rather than guessed onto a document |

**Two cells in the migration are derived rather than transcribed, and both are on `users`**,
created for the expense claims (§7): `users.email` (a UNIQUE NOT NULL column, and no captured
file holds an email address) and `users.password_hash` (`'!'`, which is not a bcrypt hash, so
the account cannot log in). The email is synthesised from the captured name —
`lower(replace(contact, ' ', '.')) || '@demo.local'` — so `Xero Demo` becomes
`xero.demo@demo.local`. Nothing else in the file is derived.

The one *number* that is not a straight copy is the tax split inside a bank transaction's
journal: Xero's bank grid prints a single gross amount, so the coded account gets
`round(|amount| / (1 + rate), 2)` and 820 gets the remainder. That is reconciliation.md §2's
rule, and it is verified rather than asserted — it is the only split under which 820 lands on
Xero's 422.59 and 090 on 7,430.22.

### The document rows and the ledger agree

The migration asserts this before it commits, and the assertion failed twice during
development before it was right (see §11), so it is a real check:

```
NOTICE:  aged receivables: documents 9194.5100 = ledger 610 9194.5100
NOTICE:  aged payables:    documents 8386.7600 = ledger 800 8386.7600
NOTICE:  aged expense claims: documents 115.9500 = ledger 801 115.9500
```

The document side applies the filter the aged reports apply — `status = 'AUTHORISED' AND
amount_due > 0`. Without it the 2 DRAFT sales invoices and the DRAFT/VOIDED/DELETED bills
would count as receivables and payables, which is exactly what the ledger refuses to do.

Expense claims side by side, as asked:

lost: it is in `gl_journal_lines` under `source_type = 'EXPENSECLAIM'` (11 lines: the
account, description and tax of every captured line).

### The expense claims' claimant: two names for one claim, left alone

* `migrations/data/xero/expense-claims.csv` line 2, and Xero's own General Ledger Detail
  (`docs/xero-reference/general-ledger-detail.txt`, section "##Unpaid Expense Claims", line
  `10 Sep 2026  Expense Claim  Xero Demo  -  115.95  (115.95)  -  0.00%`) both call the
  claimant **Xero Demo**.
* Xero's Aged Payables Summary (`docs/xero-reference/aged-payables-summary.txt`, the
  "Expense Claims" block) calls the same 115.95 **Adam Michkevich**.

The row is stored with the CSV's name, `Xero Demo`, because requirement 3 says every value
comes from a CSV row and "Adam Michkevich" appears in **no** captured CSV. No mapping between
the two names is guessed and nothing is invented to make them agree. **This is an open
difference for the aged-payables work to reproduce or declare**: the block will print
`Xero Demo` where Xero prints `Adam Michkevich`, with the same 115.95.

`users.email` is UNIQUE NOT NULL and `password_hash` is NOT NULL, and no captured file holds
either. The two claimants are created as `users` rows — `Xero Demo`
(`xero.demo@demo.local`) and `Orlena Greenville` (`orlena.greenville@demo.local`) — with
`first_name`/`last_name` split from the CSV's own `contact` value, so
`internal/repository/report.go::AgedExpenseClaims` (which reads
`COALESCE(NULLIF(TRIM(CONCAT_WS(' ', first_name, last_name)), ''), email, 'Unassigned
expense claim')`) prints the CSV's name back. `password_hash = '!'` cannot be logged in with.
No `organisation_users` row is written: the claim's own `organisation_id` scopes the users,
and a membership row would add two strangers to the members list, which no captured file
shows.

| Tax ID number | `tax_number` | *(empty)* | `101-2-303` | |

### Settings the schema cannot hold

`organisations` has no column for any of these, and `00024` does **not** add one or smuggle
them into `profile`:

* **Tax basis: Accrual Basis** — there is no tax-basis column.
* **Tax period: 3 Monthly** — there is no filing-period column.
* **Tax ID display name: `Tax reg`** — `tax_number` holds the number, not its label.
* **Town/City `Marineville`, Postal code `12345`, Telephone `800 1234 5678`** — the org has
  no address or phone columns; `contact_addresses` / `contact_phones` are per-contact and
  both require a `type`, and the organisation is not a contact.
* **Organisation type / industry** — `organisation_type` exists (default `COMPANY`) but
  org-settings.txt publishes no value to write into it.

## 11. Other differences worth naming

* **`invoices` numbering constraint.** `invoices` had `UNIQUE(organisation_id,
  invoice_number)`, which Xero's captured bills break — three bills numbered `AP`, three
  `RENT`, six `Rpt`, five unnumbered. `00024` drops it and replaces it with the same
  uniqueness limited to the side that really has it:
  `CREATE UNIQUE INDEX invoices_org_accrec_number_key ON invoices (organisation_id,
  invoice_number) WHERE type = 'ACCREC' AND invoice_number IS NOT NULL;`. The `Down` half
  drops the index and puts the constraint back. No Go code references the constraint by name.
* **`short_code` and the test suite.** `internal/repository/integration_test.go` asserted
  `DEMO`; `00024` writes Xero's `!!6Sp3`, so that assertion was updated to expect `!!6Sp3`
  with a comment naming the migration. No other place pins the old value — a tree-wide grep
  for `DEMO` finds only `00009_seed_demo.sql` (the seed this migration overwrites),
  `00023`'s and `00024`'s own comments, `00024`'s `Down` half, and `DEMO-5xxx` document
  numbers in `migrations/fixtures/`, which are unrelated. **The test suite was not run**: the
  integration tests use the test database on `:5433`, which this task forbids touching.
* **The Sales Tax figure 897.35** mentioned in the dispatch does not appear in any captured
  file, in `docs/xero-reference/reconciliation.md`, or in the ledger, so it cannot be tied
  out here. What the captures do support is account 820:
  `output tax on sales (credits) 2,544.62`, `input tax on purchases (debits) 2,122.03`,
  `net 820 liability 422.59` — which is reconciliation.md §3's row for 820 (109 postings,
  2,122.03 Dr / 2,544.62 Cr).
* **Ageing buckets differ, per-contact totals do not.** goXero buckets by days (≤0 / 1–30 /
  31–60 / 61–90 / >90); Xero buckets by whole months (< 1 / 1 / 2 / 3 / Older). Every one of
  the 6 AR contacts and all 11 AP contacts reaches Xero's total to the cent; the column a
  given row lands in can differ. Example: PC Complete 2,132.51 is due 2026-10-11, i.e. 81
  days past due at 31 Dec 2026, so it sits in goXero's 61–90 column where Xero prints it
  under "2 Months". Aged Receivables, documents side:

  ```
  Contact                            Total due   (Xero)
  Basket Case                          914.55    914.55
  Bayside Club                         234.00    234.00
  City Limousines                     1191.83   1191.83
  DIISR - Small Business Services      270.63    270.63
  Marine Systems                       396.00    396.00
  Ridgeway University                 6187.50   6187.50
                                      -------   -------
                                      9194.51   9194.51
  ```

  Aged Payables, documents side (11 contacts, 12 bills): Bayside Club 130.00, Bayside
  Wholesale 840.00, Capital Cab Co 242.00, Central Copiers 163.56, Net Connect 54.13,
  PC Complete 2,132.51, PowerDirect 108.60, SMART Agency 4,500.00 (2,000.00 + 2,500.00),
  Swanston Security 59.54, Xero 31.39, Young Bros Transport 125.03 — **total 8,386.76**,
  matching `aged-payables-summary.txt`'s `Total Aged Payables`.
* **`bank_transaction_line_items.account_code` on the 26 settlement rows** is the *control*
  account the ledger clears (800 / 610 / 801), not the account the capture records, which is
  the paid document's own account. The captured value is preserved in the line's
  description as `... (Xero coded this row to <code>)`. See `docs/xero-reference/README.md`'s
  method notes: Xero's grid resolves the coding by hopping to the linked bill, while its
  ledger clears the control account. This is the specific reason `00023`, which posted every
  row to the captured code, could not reach Xero's Trial Balance.
* **`contact.type`.** `contacts.csv`'s `type` column is Xero's own flag ("customer",
  "supplier", "customer;supplier", or empty). The schema has only the two booleans, so an
  empty type means neither flag rather than an invented one: 22 of the 54 contacts end up
  with neither.
* **Three assertions failed while this migration was being written, and each one was a real
  defect, not a tolerance**: the trial-balance total was first computed by summing raw lines
  of the 25 accounts instead of netting them (108,142.54 against 42,595.46); the
  document/ledger check first counted DRAFT and VOIDED documents as receivables and payables;
  and the bank sub_total/ total_tax split first took the document's sign from the ledger leg,
  which is the opposite sign. All three are now asserted inside the migration, so the
  migration cannot commit while any of them is wrong.
